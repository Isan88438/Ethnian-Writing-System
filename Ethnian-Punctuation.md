# Ethnian Punctuation

## Overview
Ethnian uses punctuation to organize written language, indicate pauses, and distinguish quoted speech from surrounding text. Its punctuation system is designed to remain simple and consistent across languages.

| Latin | Ethnian | Type | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Full stop (`.`) | `.` | Punctuation | Ends a declarative sentence or statement. |
| Comma (`,`) | `,` | Punctuation | Indicates a short pause or separates items in a list. |
| Single Quotation Marks (`‘ ’`)<br>Double Quotation Marks (`“ ”`)<br>Straight Double Quotation Marks (`" "`)<br>Straight Single Quotation Marks (`' '`) | `' '` | Punctuation | Enclose direct speech, quotations, or cited text. |

### Spacing Rules

* No space is placed before a full stop or comma.
* One space normally follows a full stop or comma when the sentence continues.
* Quotation marks are placed directly before and after the quoted material.
